About

I'm a Computer Science student focused on applied AI, specifically retrieval-augmented generation systems and the unglamorous plumbing that makes them actually useful: scraping, parsing, chunking, embedding, caching.

I work across the full stack, from data pipelines and vector databases to frontend interfaces, and I care a lot about software quality. A large part of my coursework and project work has been formal testing: designing test suites, running exploratory and regression passes, and documenting findings.

Currently building a RAG-based university assistant as my thesis project.
